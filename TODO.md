# iConnect BLR delivery outcomes

- [ ] Public landing page clearly positions iConnect BLR as a Bengaluru mobile shop offering buy, sell, exchange, and repair, and identifies RT Nagar and Yeshwanthpur branches.
- [ ] Product browsing supports search, category filtering, product cards with price/condition/storage, and add-to-cart behavior.
- [ ] Cart and checkout flow let a customer review items, choose a branch, and reach a clearly labelled dummy/demo payment step with a success state; no real payment is processed.
- [ ] Customer login action uses the real Manus OAuth handoff supplied by the starter rather than simulated credentials.
- [ ] Sell-your-phone form collects customer contact details, device model, condition, storage, issue notes, preferred branch, and photo upload, then confirms submission.
- [ ] Repair booking form supports device type, model, issue, preferred branch, name, phone, and email, and communicates the repair workflow and trust features.
- [ ] Page includes authentic public business references: Instagram handle @iconnect.blr, RT Nagar and Yeshwanthpur locations, and service categories from iConnect's public repair site.
- [ ] Layout is polished, responsive, accessible, and includes meaningful empty/success states and usable contact actions.
